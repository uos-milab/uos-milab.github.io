Open Positions

We are always looking for self-motivated and passionate PhD/MS students, Postdocs, and Undergraduate students to join our MILab!

We accept applications throughout the year. However, the number of available positions may vary each semester depending on the lab's circumstances. Please note that we may not have openings every semester.

The timing of interviews may also vary depending on the lab's situation, so we encourage prospective applicants to prepare and apply early.

  1. Application Process
    • Please apply by email with your CV and academic transcript.
    • After reviewing the documents, we will contact you individually to schedule an interview.
    • During the interview, we will have a brief discussion about your research interests, previous experience, and general knowledge of AI and machine learning.
  2. Prerequisites / Preferred Qualifications
    • Linear algebra, probability, and statistics theory
    • Proficient in implementing code with Python programming
    • (Preference) Machine learning course attendees or high English score holders
  3. Benefits
    • Stipend provided (paid during participation in funded research projects, with differences according to the degree program).
    • Incentives provided for publishing in top-tier conferences/journals and support for attending international conferences.
    • Coffee from a café is provided.☕
